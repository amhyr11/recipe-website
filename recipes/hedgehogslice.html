Hedgehog Slice

This one of Australia's best kept secrets. I can't believe I've lived here for so long without trying it... til now! Let's make this delicious treat together.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 1/4 cup cocoa powder
  • 2 cups crushed biscuits
  • 1 cup chopped nuts
  • 1 cup desiccated coconut
  • 1/2 cup condensed milk

Instructions

  1. In a saucepan, melt the butter and sugar together over low heat.
  2. Add the cocoa powder and stir until well combined.
  3. Remove from heat and stir in the crushed biscuits, chopped nuts, desiccated coconut, and condensed milk until everything is well coated.
  4. Press the mixture into a lined baking dish and refrigerate for at least 2 hours or until firm.
  5. Once set, cut into slices and serve.
